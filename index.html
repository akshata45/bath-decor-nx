Welcome to Bath Decor NX

Crafting timeless spaces with design, detail, and distinction since 2001.

Born from a passion for artistic surfaces, Bath Decor NX began its journey in 2001 with a vision to transform ordinary spaces into extraordinary experiences. What started as a small team is now a growing legacy across India.

Our approach goes beyond products — we curate expressions. From designer tiles to complete lifestyle solutions, every selection reflects elegance, innovation, and a deep understanding of modern luxury.

With decades of trust and a strong retail presence, we continue to set new benchmarks in design, quality, and customer experience — shaping spaces that truly reflect individuality.
