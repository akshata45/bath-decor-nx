Bath Decor NX

Transforming spaces with designer tiles and luxury solutions since 2001.

Bath Decor NX began with a vision to redefine how spaces are designed through premium tiles and curated interiors. What started as a small team has grown into a trusted brand with 25+ years of experience, serving customers across India.

With multiple retail stores and franchises, we offer a wide range of designer tiles, bath fittings, and interior solutions for homes, offices, and commercial spaces. Our focus is on innovation, quality, and delivering a personalized experience that turns every space into something unique and elegant.
